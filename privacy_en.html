2. Types of Data Collected

DriveKeep is designed to process your data primarily locally on your device. No automatic synchronization with an external cloud takes place.

  • User Interactions: Information about your vehicles, refuels, and maintenance is entered by you and stored locally in a database on your device.
  • Media Content: When you import photos (e.g., fuel receipts) or PDF documents, the app accesses this data. This remains under your control on your device.
  • Optical Character Recognition (OCR): The app uses technologies for text recognition from images. This processing takes place locally on your device to protect your privacy.
  • Technical Data: We collect basic information such as the operating system, device type, and app version to ensure the stability of the app.

3. Permissions and Access

The app requests certain permissions that are necessary for the core functions:

  • Storage Access: Required to access files, images, and PDFs that you want to import into the app for management.
  • Camera: Required to take photos directly from the app.
  • Notifications: Allows the app to load schedules in the background to remind you of maintenance or appointments.
  • In-App Purchases: Required for the billing of premium features via the Google Play Store.

4. Third-Party Services and Advertising

To keep the app advertiser-funded and functional, we work with the following third parties:

  • Google AdMob: We use Google Ads to display advertising. This may involve processing of advertising IDs and other device information. These data are transmitted to Google, which may involve transfer to third countries (e.g., USA).
  • Google Play Billing: For the purchase of in-app features, data is transmitted to the Google Play Store.

You can restrict the use of advertising IDs in the device settings of your mobile phone.

5. Data Transfer to Third Countries

By using services like Google AdMob, data is transmitted to countries outside the EU (especially the USA). We rely on the Standard Contractual Clauses of the EU Commission to ensure an appropriate level of data protection.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the data stored about you.
  • Correction of incorrect data.
  • Deletion of your data (by deleting the app or manually deleting within the app).
  • Restriction of processing.
  • Objection to processing for advertising purposes.

Contact us for corresponding requests at the email address provided above.

7. Security

We use technical measures to protect your data from unauthorized access. Since the data is stored locally on your device, the responsibility for device security (e.g. display lock, password protection) lies with you.
